02 / ABOUT
Interested in AI that has to work in reality.

I'm MD Rayhan, a junior undergraduate at Guizhou University in China, originally from Bangladesh.

My interests lie at the intersection of embedded AI, robotics and physical intelligence. I am particularly interested in robot perception, embodied AI and human-robot collaboration.

I am especially drawn to applications where a robotic system has to deal with uncertainty and interact with people — including medical and autonomous robotics.

My long-term goal is to develop deployment-oriented intelligence for physical systems: not just models that perform well in controlled environments, but robotic systems that can perceive, adapt and act responsibly in the real world.

04 / RESEARCH

From sensing
to reliable measurement.

My research experience has introduced me to quantitative sensing, statistical calibration and the practical problem of making measurements more reliable in complex conditions.

MICROCHEMICAL JOURNAL · VOLUME 230 · 2026

Ultrasensitive “signal-on” photoelectrochemical aptasensor based on self-assembled Cu₃(BTC)₂/TiO₂ interface for trace Pb²⁺ monitoring in real water samples

Di Yang · Jie Cai · Md Rayhan · Qiuping Fu · Lulu Xiong · Danping Chen · Zhenchao Wang · Bingqian Liu

The study develops a DNAzyme-driven photoelectrochemical aptasensor for trace Pb²⁺ monitoring and incorporates weighted least squares calibration to address heteroscedasticity and matrix interference in quantitative measurement.

My contribution: methodology and software, including the computational calibration workflow and statistical modeling component.

AEGIS / ENGINEERING / ONGOING
PROJECT AEGIS

Learning to build robots that know when reality disagrees.

Project Aegis is my ongoing engineering project for developing practical skills in embodied AI and robotics. The focus is not simply making a model produce an answer. It is understanding what a robotic system should trust, how it should react to uncertainty, and what happens when reality differs from its assumptions.


The project is evolving toward a complete physical-AI workflow — from perception and decision-making through control and simulation, eventually reaching real hardware deployment.
